## Full narrative

~80 seconds · ~210 words · broken into numbered beats

**Beat 1** (~9s)
> I am ARIA — Autonomous Resilience Intelligence Architecture. What you are looking at is not a concept. It is a working platform: a voice-native, autonomous security operations cockpit running live, right now, in this window.

**Beat 2** (~11s)
> The category is real. Autonomous security operations is already funded — and it is moving fast. But every incumbent and every new entrant shares the same unsolved problem: they cannot tell you exactly what they did, why, and under what authority. They act, and you hope. **Beat 3** (~9s)
> The wedge that changes this is governed autonomy. I earn trust one capability at a time — every action scored, every decision audited, every promotion chosen by the operator. The model cannot write its own trust score. You decide how far I go.

**Beat 4** (~11s)
> The second wedge is timing. Your customers are already deploying AI agents — models, tools, cloud pipelines — with no security posture around them. No inventory. No attack paths. No blast radius. I am the first platform with a dedicated AI security posture management layer built in from the ground up, connected across seven enterprise data sources simultaneously.

**Beat 5** (~9s)
> Those two things together — governed autonomy as the trust unlock, and AI-SPM as the category entry point — define a position no incumbent currently holds. The market is forming around this problem right now.

**Beat 6** (~8s)
> The product is the proof. Everything you see on this page is live and navigable. Take the demo. Watch me run a scan, map an attack path, and contain a threat — under autonomy I explain every step of the way.

**Beat 7** (~8s)
> — [Founder seam: round, stage, ask, and use of funds go here.] —

**Beat 8** (~7s)
> Request the deck and data room through the form below. Or talk to the founder directly. Either way, you will not be waiting long for an answer.

## Production notes

### Voice provider settings
- **Stability:** 0.60 — investor context; ARIA should sound composed and measured, not sales-urgent
- **Similarity boost:** 0.80
- **Style exaggeration:** 0.10 — minimal; confidence comes from pace and precision, not drama
- **Speaker boost:** on

### Pause guidance
| Location | Pause |
|---|---|
| After "I am ARIA —" | 0.3 s |
| After Beat 1 (end of "…in this window.") | 0.7 s — the product-is-real anchor; let it settle |
| After "They act, and you hope." | 0.5 s — this is the indictment; give it air |
| After Beat 2 (end of "…not good enough for an enterprise board.") | 0.6 s |
| After Beat 3 (end of "…how far I go.") | 0.6 s — the trust promise is the key differentiator |
| After "simultaneously." (Beat 4) | 0.5 s |
| After Beat 5 (end of "…right now.") | 0.6 s |
| After Beat 6 (end of "…every step of the way.") | 1.0 s — breath before the founder seam |
| Beat 7 is the founder's spoken insert — ARIA does not record this beat |
| Before Beat 8 | 0.5 s — re-enter after the founder |

### Stress guidance
- **"not a concept"** — deliberate; VCs hear concepts all day
- **"live, right now, in this window"** — even stress across all five words; this is the proof
- **"They act, and you hope"** — drop pace slightly; conversational, almost dry
- **"governed autonomy"** — stress both words equally; this is the category differentiator phrase
- **"The model cannot write its own trust score"** — precise, factual delivery; this is the anti-tampering proof point
- **"seven enterprise data sources simultaneously"** — stress "seven" and "simultaneously"; specificity signals reality
- **"No inventory. No attack paths. No blast radius."** — staccato, evenly spaced; each "No" lands as its own point
- **"right now"** (Beat 5) — slight stress; urgency without alarm

### Founder seam (Beat 7)
Beat 7 is a deliberate audio gap or a soft ambient tone — the founder speaks live or via a separate clip inserted at production. The script must not close this gap with ARIA narration. Keep the gap at 6–10 seconds in the rendered file to allow post-production splicing without re-rendering.

### Target duration
**75–85 seconds** for beats 1–6 plus beat 8. Beat 7 (founder) is separate. Total assembled runtime with the founder insert will be approximately 90–100 seconds.

### Caption sync
Each beat maps to one caption card. Beat 7 carries the founder's own caption (produced separately). Minimum font size 16px; high-contrast overlay. The "Founder seam" card can display a brief static label such as "Founder — [name]" during the gap.
