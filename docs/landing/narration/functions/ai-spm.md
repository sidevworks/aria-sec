# AI-SPM — AI Security Posture Management

> Narration for the "Find out more" deep-dive on ARIA's lead capability:
> discovering and securing the AI systems companies are already running.

---

## Teaser

Your company is already running AI agents. Most have no security posture around them. I discover every model, prompt, secret, and cloud asset — then map exactly how an attacker would reach them, and what it would cost you if they did.

*(~35 words / ~11 seconds)*

---

## Full narrative

**Beat 1 — The why-now**
There is a security gap that is widening right now, in almost every organization. AI agents, language models, and automated pipelines are being deployed faster than security teams can track them. They carry prompts that encode business logic, secrets that open cloud infrastructure, and model endpoints with no access controls. I exist, in part, to close that gap — before it becomes an incident.

**Beat 2 — What I discover**
I connect to your environment across seven sources simultaneously: GitHub, AWS, Okta, Azure Active Directory, Snyk, VirusTotal, and Elastic. Across those connectors, I discover AI systems, prompts, tools, endpoints, secrets, model infrastructure, cloud assets, and identity links. Every finding is normalized and deduplicated into a single inventory so you are never looking at duplicates or gaps.

**Beat 3 — The attack path**
Discovery is only the beginning. For every meaningful finding, I generate an attack narrative: a summary of what the exposure is, the most likely path an attacker would take to exploit it, my confidence in that assessment, the blast radius — meaning the downstream systems and data that would be affected — and the specific remediation actions I recommend. You are not reading a raw finding list. You are reading a threat story with a resolution.

**Beat 4 — The risk engine**
Every finding carries a severity score — critical, high, medium, or low — with CVSS normalization and a plain-language rationale for why it was rated that way. The underlying graph store maps relationships between assets, so I can answer questions like: which AI secrets have a path to production infrastructure, and how many hops does it take? You can ask me that in plain language, and I will answer it directly.

**Beat 5 — The lifecycle**
Findings do not disappear once you acknowledge them. I track every action taken against a finding — whether you acted on it, resolved it, ignored it with a reason, or reopened it after a change. I can generate a remediation plan, open a sandboxed evidence environment so you can safely explore a threat without exposing the organization, produce a scan report, and schedule recurring scans so posture stays current as your environment changes.

**Beat 6 — Why this is the right time**
This is not a future problem. It is the problem your security team faces today, with tools that were built before AI agents existed. I was built for this moment.

*(~185 words / ~62 seconds)*

## Production notes

**Voice guidance:**
- Beat 1: open with a slight gravity in the tone. "Before it becomes an incident" — land this with a deliberate pause before it.
- Beat 2: the list of seven connectors (GitHub, AWS, Okta…) should be read evenly and clearly, not rushed. Each connector name should be distinct.
- Beat 3: "blast radius" — slight emphasis, as it is a signature term. Pause briefly after "what the exposure is" before continuing the list.
- Beat 5: faster and more matter-of-fact — this is operational capability, not drama.
- Beat 6: slower again. The final sentence is a closer. Pause before "I was built for this moment."

**Target duration:** 60–68 seconds for the full narrative.

**Caption sync points:** each beat maps to a distinct visual sequence — discovery graph populating (beat 2), attack path drawing itself (beat 3), severity scores appearing (beat 4), findings lifecycle timeline (beat 5).
