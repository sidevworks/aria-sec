# Galaxy — The ARIA Command Interface

> Narration for the "Find out more" deep-dive on the galaxy cockpit itself:
> how the interface works, how to move through it, and what the live color system means.

---

## Teaser

I am ARIA. Every capability you see here is a live sector in my galaxy — a command interface that navigates like a cockpit, not a dashboard. Click, speak, type, or let me take you there.

*(~30 words / ~10 seconds)*

---

## Full narrative

**Beat 1 — What you are looking at**
I am ARIA — Autonomous Resilience Intelligence Architecture. What you are navigating right now is not a menu system. It is a spatial command interface: every sector of my capability is a luminous hub in this galaxy, and every node inside that hub is a live capability you can enter.

**Beat 2 — Four ways to move**
You can move through this environment four ways, and they are completely interchangeable. Click any sector or node directly. Speak a destination — I will open it. Type a command into the console bar. Or let me route you automatically when a critical event fires and demands your attention.

**Beat 3 — The color system**
Every node and sector you see carries a live semantic color. Blue means nominal — the system is healthy and the signal is clean. Amber means I have detected a threat condition that deserves your attention. Red, or magenta, means critical: I have identified an active risk and am ready to act. These colors are not decorative. They update continuously from my scanning engines, so the state of your environment is readable at a glance, from anywhere in the galaxy.

**Beat 4 — The persistent cockpit**
The chrome surrounding the galaxy never disappears. The top sector bar keeps every major capability one click away. The left status stack tells you my current threat level, autonomy mode, and how many decisions are waiting for your review. The right rail gives you the voice toggle, the guided tour, the audio controls, and direct access to the command console — always within reach, no matter where you are in the experience.

**Beat 5 — Why this matters**
Most security platforms are collections of separate tools. I am a single coherent environment. A signal detected in the network sector is the same signal the decision engine reasons over, the same signal the identity galaxy visualizes, and the same signal I can explain to you in plain language the moment you ask. The galaxy is not the interface to the product. It is the product.

*(~180 words / ~60 seconds)*

---

## Production notes

**Voice guidance:**
- Pace: measured and authoritative throughout. ARIA is never rushed.
- Beat 1: slight emphasis on "not a menu system" — this is the reframe.
- Beat 3: pause after each color tier ("Blue… [0.4s pause] …Amber… [0.4s pause] …Red"). Let each land visually before moving to the next.
- Beat 5: "The galaxy is not the interface to the product. It is the product." — slow down on the final sentence. Full stop pause before the last four words.
- No uptalk. Declarative statements only.

**Target duration:** 55–65 seconds for the full narrative.

**Caption sync points:** each numbered beat maps to a distinct visual state — use beat boundaries as caption segment breaks.
